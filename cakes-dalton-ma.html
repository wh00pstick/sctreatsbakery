✦ Dalton, MA ✦

Custom Cakes & Treats
Delivered to Dalton

Dalton is essentially next door — well under twenty minutes east of Kelsey’s Pittsfield kitchen, with Hinsdale just beyond. It’s one of the most flexible deliveries on the board, which means short-notice requests from Dalton get a yes more often than from anywhere else in the county.

✦ Close to Home ✦

The Easiest Delivery
in the Berkshires

Most Dalton orders are for family celebrations at home rather than big venues: a birthday cake, a dozen cupcakes for a classroom, a brunch box for visiting relatives, a pan of cinnamon buns for a Sunday morning. Delivery is rarely the constraint on a Dalton order — lead time is.

Being close by doesn’t shorten how long a cake takes to bake, chill, fill, and decorate, so a custom cake still needs about a week. What proximity buys you is flexibility: a Dalton delivery is far easier to slot into a full day than a two-hour round trip, and cupcakes, cookies, and brunch boxes can often go out with 24–48 hours’ notice.

Choose from 14 scratch-made cake flavors and 14 frostings — vanilla and funfetti classics to lemon raspberry, pumpkin, and mocha. See the full flavor menu → Gluten-free flavors are available on request.

✦ Good to Know ✦

Dalton Delivery Questions

Can you do something on short notice for Dalton?

More often than anywhere else. Cupcakes, cookies, brownies, and brunch boxes need 24–48 hours; a custom cake still needs about a week. If your party is this weekend, ask — the answer depends on the calendar, not the distance.

What does delivery to Dalton cost?

Fees are quoted by distance with your order. Dalton is among the shortest runs from Pittsfield, so it sits at the very low end. Pickup in Pittsfield is free and only a few minutes away.

Do you deliver to Hinsdale, too?

Yes. Hinsdale is just past Dalton and counts as one of the close-in, flexible towns.
