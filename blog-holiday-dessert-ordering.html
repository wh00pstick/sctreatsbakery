Ordering Holiday Desserts:
A Timeline

Every year, the same thing: a rush of messages the week of Thanksgiving asking for something by Thursday. Here's the schedule that actually works.

Holiday baking is the one time of year where lead times stop being a formality. A home bakery has a fixed number of oven hours, and in late November and December they go fast. Ordering early isn't about being organized — it's the difference between getting what you want and getting whatever's still possible.

The weeks that fill first

  • The week of Thanksgiving — specifically Tuesday and Wednesday pickups. This is the single busiest stretch of the fall.
  • The two weeks before Christmas — cookie trays, party desserts, and gifts all land at once.
  • December 23rd and 24th — effectively spoken for well in advance.
  • New Year's Eve — smaller volume, but a narrow window.

Rule of thumb: for Thanksgiving, reach out by early November. For Christmas, reach out by the end of November. Both are earlier than feels necessary, and both are the reason people get what they wanted.

What to order for a hosted dinner

If you're bringing dessert to someone else's table, you want something that travels well, needs no assembly, and doesn't require oven space at the destination — because the oven will not be available.

  • Fudgy brownies ($20 per half-dozen) and chocolate chip cookies ($18 per dozen) — the most forgiving things to transport, and they hold for days.
  • Chocolate-dipped strawberries ($18 per half-dozen) — the one that looks like you tried hardest for the least effort.
  • Cupcakes ($30 per dozen) — individually portioned, no cutting, no plates.
  • A custom cake (from $70) if you're the one hosting and want a centerpiece.
  • Cinnamon rolls ($6 each, $32 per half-dozen) — the morning-after order, and a genuinely great thing to have in the house on Christmas morning.

Seasonal flavors

Fall and winter is when the warm-spice side of the menu comes out: pumpkin, apple, spice cake, and carrot all sit in the standard 14 cake flavors, and pair naturally with cream cheese, caramel, honey, or toasted marshmallow frosting. Seasonal donuts run $22 per dozen when they're on.

Flavors do rotate through the season, so it's always worth asking what's available for your date rather than assuming.

If you're hosting out-of-town family

Berkshire households swell at the holidays. If you're feeding a group larger than usual, work out your real headcount before you order — the serving size guide covers how to size a cake for a crowd, and mixing a smaller cake with a tray of cookies usually feeds more people for less than a single very large cake.
