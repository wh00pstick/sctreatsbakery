✦ North Adams, MA ✦

Custom Cakes
Delivered to North Adams

North Adams is twenty to forty minutes north of Kelsey’s Pittsfield kitchen, with Cheshire and Adams on the way. Family birthdays, showers, and holiday orders are the bread and butter here — and because the run passes straight through Adams, orders for both towns are often on the same trip.

✦ North County ✦

Birthdays, Showers
& Sunday Brunch

Most North Adams orders are for celebrations at home: a themed birthday cake for a kid’s party, a dozen cupcakes for the office, a brunch box or a pan of cinnamon buns for a Sunday with family. Cupcakes and treats need just 24–48 hours; a custom cake needs about a week.

As with anywhere in North County, winter road conditions matter more than mileage. A snowy-day delivery over the hills gets scheduled with extra room and, on a genuinely bad day, rescheduled by phone rather than risked.

Choose from 14 scratch-made cake flavors and 14 frostings — vanilla and funfetti classics to lemon raspberry, pumpkin, and mocha. See the full flavor menu → Gluten-free flavors are available on request.

✦ Good to Know ✦

North Adams Delivery Questions

How much notice do you need for North Adams?

24–48 hours for cupcakes, cookies, brownies, brunch boxes, and cinnamon buns. About a week for a custom cake. 2–4 weeks for weddings and cake bar events.

What does delivery to North Adams cost?

Fees are quoted by distance with your order, before you commit. North Adams is a mid-range run from Pittsfield. Pickup in Pittsfield is always free.

Do you deliver to Adams and Cheshire too?

Yes. Both are on the route north, and Cheshire is close enough to count as one of the easy, flexible deliveries.

Can I get gluten-free in North Adams?

Yes. Ten gluten-free cake flavors are available on request for an additional charge. What to know about gluten-free orders →
