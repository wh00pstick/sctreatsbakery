✦ Williamstown, MA ✦

Custom Cakes
Delivered to Williamstown

Williamstown is twenty to forty minutes north of Kelsey’s Pittsfield kitchen, depending on the season. Family birthdays, Williams College graduations and reunions, and treats sent to students from home all make the trip up Route 7 through Lanesborough and New Ashford.

✦ North County ✦

College Weekends
& Family Milestones

Commencement, reunion weekend, and parents’ weekend fill Williamstown fast, so cakes for those dates should be ordered well ahead. Cupcakes and cookie boxes are an easy way to feed a crowd of relatives without cutting anything.

One honest note about the North County drive: winter changes the math more than distance does. A February delivery over the northern hills gets scheduled with extra room, and occasionally rescheduled outright if the roads are genuinely bad — which is a conversation, never a surprise.

✦ Good to Know ✦

Williamstown Delivery Questions

How far in advance should I order for Williamstown?

24–48 hours for cupcakes and treats, about a week for a custom cake, 2–4 weeks for weddings. Graduation and reunion weekends fill early, so reach out as soon as you know your date.

What does delivery to Williamstown cost?

Delivery fees are quoted by distance with your order and scale with the drive. Williamstown is a mid-range run. Pickup in Pittsfield is free.

Can you deliver to a Williams College dorm or event?

Yes. Kelsey is happy to deliver to campus or anywhere in town: a residence hall, an off-campus house, or an event space. Include the exact building and a contact number with your order so the handoff is easy.

What about winter deliveries?

North County winter deliveries are scheduled with extra buffer. If the roads over the hills are genuinely unsafe, Kelsey will call to reschedule rather than risk the cake.
