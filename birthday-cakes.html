✦ Good to Know ✦

Birthday Cake Questions

How much notice do you need for a birthday cake?

Custom birthday cakes need about 1 week of lead time. Cupcakes, cookies, and other party treats need 24–48 hours. Rush orders are considered case-by-case — it never hurts to ask.

How much do birthday cakes cost?

Custom celebration cakes start at $70, themed character cakes at $75, and the first-birthday Smash Cake + Cupcake Set at $55. Party cupcakes are $30 per dozen. Kelsey confirms final pricing based on your size and design.

Can you match a theme or an inspiration photo?

Yes — bring your theme or an inspo picture and Kelsey will bring it to life in cake form. Mermaids, superheroes, Mickey Mouse, sunflowers, florals — custom design is what she does best.

What flavors can I choose from?

14 cake flavors — from vanilla, chocolate, and funfetti to lemon raspberry, mocha, and carrot — plus 14 frostings. See the full menu → Gluten-free flavors are available upon request for an additional charge.

Do you deliver birthday cakes?

Pickup is in Pittsfield, MA, and delivery is available across the Berkshires, Western Massachusetts, Eastern New York, Southern Vermont, and Northwest Connecticut — roughly a 1–2 hour radius. Delivery fees vary by distance.
