✦ Lenox, MA ✦

Custom Cakes & Treats
Delivered to Lenox

Lenox is one of the closest towns to Kelsey’s Pittsfield kitchen — about fifteen to twenty minutes door to door — which makes it one of the easiest deliveries on the board. Custom cakes, cupcakes, cinnamon buns, and full wedding dessert tables travel here all year, from summer rentals in Tanglewood season to family birthdays in February.

✦ Weddings & Summer Events ✦

Lenox Venues,
Handled

Lenox has the highest concentration of wedding venues in the county — Tanglewood, The Mount, Wheatleigh, Blantyre, and the Miraval Berkshires and Wyndhurst Manor properties among them. Kelsey delivers wedding cakes and dessert tables to venues, inns, and private homes throughout town. Resort venues with staffed event teams and real loading access are among the easiest deliveries in the region; estate venues with long walks from the parking area get a little extra time built in.

One Lenox-specific note: July and August are Tanglewood season. On concert days Route 183 and West Street back up badly, so a summer delivery is scheduled around the performance calendar, not the clock. More on delivering to Berkshires venues →

✦ Good to Know ✦

Lenox Delivery Questions

How far in advance should I order for Lenox?

Cupcakes, cookies, brownies, and cinnamon buns need 24–48 hours. Custom cakes need about a week. Wedding cakes and cake bar packages need 2–4 weeks. Being close by doesn’t shorten how long a cake takes to bake, chill, fill, and decorate — but it does mean short-notice requests from Lenox get a yes more often.

What does delivery to Lenox cost?

Delivery fees are quoted by distance with your order, before you commit to anything. Lenox is a short run from Pittsfield, so it sits at the low end of the range. Pickup in Pittsfield is always free.

Can you deliver to a Tanglewood wedding or event?

Yes. Kelsey delivers to venues, inns, and private homes throughout Lenox, including the estate and resort properties around Tanglewood. Summer concert weekends are scheduled early with a wider buffer because of traffic on Route 183 and West Street.

Can I pick up in Pittsfield instead?

Absolutely. Pickup is free, and for a tiered cake a short trip in your own car with someone holding the box is genuinely low-risk. The pickup address goes out when your order is confirmed.
