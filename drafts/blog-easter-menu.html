Easter Brunch:
Buns, Carrot Cake & Boxes

Easter is March 28 this year, and it’s a brunch holiday more than a dessert one. Here’s what goes on the table and when to order it.

Easter orders are almost all for the morning: something to put out when family arrives, and a sweet for after the ham. The menu leans into the two things Kelsey is best known for — cinnamon buns and carrot cake.

What past Easter menus have included

Past years’ Easter menus have offered original cinnamon buns with cream cheese frosting by the half-dozen and carrot cake cupcakes with cream cheese frosting, each with a gluten-free option. This year’s menu and prices are announced in early March on Facebook and by email to the list in the footer.

Year-round items that fit the table

  • Cinnamon buns ($6 each, $32 per half-dozen) — original, apple, maple bacon, banana bread, blueberry, or raspberry. Pick up Saturday and warm them Sunday morning.
  • Build Your Own Brunch Box (from $28) — muffins, bagels, cinnamon rolls, fruit, and more, packed so you can set it out as-is. Build a box →
  • Carrot cake — as a custom cake (from $70) or as cupcakes ($30 per dozen). Carrot is also on the gluten-free list.
  • Chocolate-dipped strawberries ($18 per half-dozen) — for the kids’ table.

Order by Wednesday, March 24. Cinnamon buns, boxes, and cupcakes need 24–48 hours; a custom cake needs about a week. Saturday-before-Easter pickups fill first.

Hosting out-of-town family?

A brunch box plus a half-dozen buns feeds six to eight people a proper breakfast without anyone cooking. If the house is fuller than usual, the serving size guide covers how to size a cake for the crowd.
