Halloween Cupcakes
& Class-Party Treats

Late October is the first real party week of the school year. Here’s what travels to a classroom, what an office spread needs, and when to get the order in.

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, which means the class parties happen Friday the 30th and the office spreads the week before. Everything on this list needs 24–48 hours, but that particular week fills, so the sooner you ask the more choice you have.

Seasonal Halloween cupcakes

Seasonal specialty cupcakes rotate through the fall — the Halloween designs are announced on Facebook and Instagram as they come out. If you have a theme or an inspiration photo, send it: custom-decorated cupcakes are the same $30 a dozen as the signature ones, and pumpkin, spice cake, and chocolate are the flavors that fit the season best. More on fall flavors →

What works for a classroom

  • Cupcakes ($30 per dozen) — individually portioned, no knife, no plates. Two dozen covers a class with a few for the teachers.
  • Chocolate chip cookies ($18 per dozen) — the safest crowd-pleaser and the easiest to carry in.
  • Fudgy brownies ($20 per half-dozen) — for the parent who was asked to bring “something chocolate.”

Ask the teacher about allergies first. Everything is baked in a licensed home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, and nuts. Gluten-free cupcakes are available on request for an additional charge, but the kitchen is not celiac-safe. What a shared kitchen means →

Office and party spreads

For an adult crowd, mix a dozen decorated cupcakes with a tray of cookies and brownies — it feeds more people for less than cupcakes alone, and the tray keeps for days. A dozen cupcakes, a dozen cookies, and six brownies runs $68 and covers about 30 people who each take one thing.

When to order

Treats need 24–48 hours any week of the year. For the week of Halloween, reach out by the weekend before. Pickup is in Pittsfield; delivery across the Berkshires is quoted by distance. Delivery towns →
