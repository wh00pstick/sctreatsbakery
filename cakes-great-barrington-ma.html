✦ Great Barrington, MA ✦

Custom Cakes
Delivered to Great Barrington

Great Barrington sits roughly thirty to forty-five minutes south of Kelsey’s Pittsfield kitchen — the far end of the in-county delivery range, but a well-worn one. Birthday cakes, shower dessert tables, and wedding cakes make the trip down Route 7 regularly, and neighboring Sheffield, Egremont, and Stockbridge are on the same run.

✦ South County ✦

Worth the Drive,
Scheduled with Care

Because South County is the longer end of the county, Great Barrington deliveries are scheduled with more margin than a run to Lenox or Dalton, and the delivery fee reflects the distance. In practice that means agreeing on a firm time window when you order and building the cake’s arrival into your day rather than the other way around.

Wedding cakes go to venues like the Thornewood Inn, which hosts up to about 140 guests, and to the farms, barns, and private homes that make up most South County weddings. Venue delivery notes →

✦ Good to Know ✦

Great Barrington Delivery Questions

How much notice do you need for a Great Barrington delivery?

The same lead times apply everywhere: 24–48 hours for cupcakes and treats, about a week for a custom cake, 2–4 weeks for weddings. Because it’s a longer run, short-notice requests are harder to slot in than they are for towns close to Pittsfield — but it never hurts to ask.

What does delivery to Great Barrington cost?

Fees are quoted by distance with your order, before you commit. Great Barrington is on the higher end of in-county delivery because of the drive. Pickup in Pittsfield is free if you’re headed north anyway.

Do you deliver to Sheffield, Egremont, and Stockbridge too?

Yes — they’re on the same route. Sheffield and Egremont run slightly longer than Great Barrington; Stockbridge is a bit shorter.

Will the cake survive a 45-minute drive?

Yes. Cakes are chilled, boxed, and driven by Kelsey herself. Tiered cakes are stacked and finished on site when the design calls for it. How cakes are transported →
